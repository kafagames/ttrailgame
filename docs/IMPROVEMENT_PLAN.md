## 2. Improved concept

**Thimble Trail** is a calm route-and-fit puzzle. Each block is a small
quilt on a cutting mat: lay rotatable fabric patches from a limited tray to
join the **spool** to the **thimble**. The tray always holds spare pieces, so
the real puzzle is *which* patches, turned *which* way, make the shortest
trail. Every block has a **par** proven by a solver.

How it plays:
1. Tap a patch in the tray (tap again to turn it).
2. Tap or drag on the quilt; a ghost shows where it lands (red thread if it can't).
3. Tap any laid patch to unpick it. Unpick / start over are always free.
4. Bind at par for ★★★, one over for ★★, otherwise ★.

## 3. Content (numbers chosen for this game)

48 blocks in 5 chapters of different lengths. 48 = a 6×8 heirloom quilt on
the hub wall, one block per level. A slow fit-puzzle suits ~50 levels rather
than 70+.

| Ch | Name | Blocks | New idea | Par range |
|----|------|--------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | First Stitches | 6 | Rail and Snip patches, turning | 1–3 |
| 2 | Turning Corners | 10 | Corner and Block patches, bends | 3–4 |
| 3 | Buttons | 9 | Buttons must be covered and joined | 4–6 |
| 4 | Matching Seams | 12 | Same fabric may not touch; pre-sewn squares | 3–4 (+rule) |
| 5 | The Heirloom | 11 | Everything, bigger frames; finale 8×8, par 7 | 4–7 |

Levels 1–2 are hand-made; 3–48 are generated by `tool/generate_levels.dart`
(winding trail through hidden waypoints → hide it with batting → add decoys →
solver proves par). `test/levels_test.dart` re-proves every par.

## 4. Retention (chosen to fit a calm puzzle)

- **Pattern of the Day** — offline, seeded by the date; +1 hint; daily streak.
- **Ribbons** — 12 local achievements, each gives a hint.
- **Hints** earned, never sold (start with 3).
- **Table covers** — 4 cosmetic cutting-table cloths unlocked by stars.
- **Ledger** — stats and ribbons.
- **The quilt itself** — the hub visibly fills block by block.
