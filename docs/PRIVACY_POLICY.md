# Privacy Policy — Thimble Trail

**Effective date:** 12 November 2026
**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **LADYKAFFY STITCHES ENTERPRISE** ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile game **Thimble Trail** (the "App"), available on Google Play, and on our website https://kafagamaereho.com.ng/. The App is developed by **Kafayat Omowunmi Ewejobi** for LADYKAFFY STITCHES ENTERPRISE, the developer named on the App's Google Play store listing.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Summary

- **We collect no data, and you never need an account.**
- We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data through the App.
- The App has **no analytics or tracking**.
- The App **works fully offline**. It does not send any data over the internet.
- The App **requests no device permissions** (no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or phone state).
- Your game progress is stored **only on your own device**, and you can delete it at any time.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data.** That includes:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details
- Location (precise or approximate)
- Device identifiers or IP addresses
- Photos, videos, audio, files, contacts or calendar data
- Usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics
- Financial or payment information
- Health, fitness or any other sensitive information

The App has no server, so no information from the App ever reaches us.

---

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves a small amount of **game data locally on your device**. This data:

- includes the quilt blocks you have sewn and their stars, hints in your tin, ribbons earned, your Pattern of the Day streak and history, simple tallies (patches sewn, quilts bound, hints used), which chapter notes you have seen, your chosen table cover, and your Quiet room settings (sound, music, vibration, reduce motion)
- contains no personal information
- is never transmitted to us or to anyone else
- is not accessible to us in any way

---

## 4. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not use, analyse, profile or process any personal data. The game data described in Section 3 is used only by the App itself, on your device, to run the game.

---

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

**We do not share, sell, rent or trade any user data with any third party**, because we do not collect any.

---

## 6. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App contains **no SDKs that collect or transmit personal data** (no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs).

The App is built with the following open-source software components. None of them collects or transmits personal data:

- **Flutter** (Google), the app framework
- **shared_preferences** (Flutter team), saves your game progress and settings on your device
- **audioplayers**, plays the game's own sound effects and music from files inside the App
- **Josefin Slab** typeface, used under the SIL Open Font License

The App is distributed through **Google Play**. When you download the App, Google may process information under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive any personal information about you from Google Play.

---

## 7. Device Permissions

The App **does not request any Android permissions**. It cannot access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, photos, phone calls or messages, and it does not need internet access to work.

---

## 8. If You Contact Us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix any problem you report. We never share it or add you to a mailing list, and we delete support emails once they are resolved and no longer needed, within 12 months at most.

---

## 9. This Website

This website does not use analytics or tracking cookies and does not collect personal data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) in server logs for security and to keep the site running. We do not use these logs to identify you.

---

## 10. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data for us to store or protect on any server. Your local game data stays inside the App's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. Support emails are kept in a password-protected mailbox that only we can access.

---

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

- **We retain no user data from the App**, because it collects none.
- Your local game data stays on your device until you delete it.
- **To delete all game data**, you can:
  1. open the App and go to **Quiet room → Unpick everything**, or
  2. uninstall the App, or
  3. clear the App's storage in your device settings (**Settings → Apps → Thimble Trail → Storage → Clear data**).

The App has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Deleted game data cannot be recovered. To have a support email deleted sooner, just ask us.

---

## 12. Children's Privacy

Thimble Trail is a calm puzzle game made for a general audience of teens and adults. **We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13** (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). No account is needed, and the App has no chat, no user-generated content and no way to contact other players.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
